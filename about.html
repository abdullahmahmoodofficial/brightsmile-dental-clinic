About Us

The clinic Lahore trusts since 2010

Two chairs, one promise: painless dentistry at an honest price. Fifteen years later, that promise built one of Lahore's most loved dental practices.

Our Story

Started small. Grew on trust alone.

BrightSmile opened in 2010 as a two-chair clinic in Gulberg. We never advertised much — we didn't need to. Patients who were treated honestly sent their families, then their whole streets.

Today we are a full specialist practice: implants, orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, laser treatments and a dedicated kids' unit — still in Gulberg, still run by the same founding doctors, and still giving a written estimate before any treatment begins.

  • We explain every option — and its price — before treatment
  • Strict 4-step sterilization on every single instrument
  • Special gentle protocol for children & nervous patients
  • Follow-up calls after every major procedure
